Affective Envelope Follower →

A Glitch Voice prototype that uses a pretrained model to estimate arousal, valence, and dominance from a live microphone. The estimates become control signals for distortion, reverb, delay, and granulation in Max/MSP.

Glitch Voice (RAVE Prototype) →

A live voice effect that uses models trained on sustained and burst-like vocal sounds. A pressure-sensitive controller lets the performer move between the two textures by changing their grip.
